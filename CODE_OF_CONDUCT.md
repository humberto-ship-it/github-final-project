# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints,
giving constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what
is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
insults, discriminatory comments, threats, unwanted attention, and publishing
another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications
that conflict with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and whenever an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project
maintainer through the contact method listed in the repository profile.
Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may apply a correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban,
depending on the impact and recurrence of the behavior. The response should be
proportionate, documented, and focused on community safety.
